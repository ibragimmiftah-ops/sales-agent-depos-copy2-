# Pricing

Prices depend on scope, integrations, channels, and required uptime. All figures below are ranges for orientation; a final estimate requires a discovery call.

## AI Sales Agent

- **MVP prototype**: €3,000 – €6,000
- **Production version**: €8,000 – €20,000
- **Monthly maintenance**: €500 – €1,500

Includes: conversation design, LLM provider setup, tool integrations (CRM + calendar), RAG for company knowledge, scoring, testing, and dashboard.

## AI Support Assistant

- **Setup**: €4,000 – €12,000
- **Monthly maintenance**: €400 – €1,000

Depends on knowledge base size, number of languages, and escalation channels.

## Lead Processing Automation

- **Setup**: €5,000 – €15,000
- **Monthly maintenance**: €600 – €1,800

Depends on lead volume, source channels, CRM complexity, and enrichment requirements.

## Telegram Bot

- **Simple bot**: €1,500 – €4,000
- **Bot + admin panel + integrations**: €5,000 – €12,000

## RAG System

- **Setup**: €4,000 – €10,000
- **Monthly vector DB + embedding costs**: €100 – €800

## CRM Integration

- **Standard CRM** (HubSpot, Bitrix24, Pipedrive): €2,000 – €6,000
- **Enterprise CRM** (Salesforce, custom): €6,000 – €18,000

## What affects the cost

1. Number of conversation scenarios and edge cases.
2. Number of integrations (CRM, calendar, messenger, internal API).
3. Languages and regions.
4. SLA and response-time requirements.
5. Compliance and data residency needs.
6. Volume of documents for RAG.

## Payment terms

- 40% upfront, 40% after acceptance testing, 20% after go-live.
- Maintenance is invoiced monthly.
