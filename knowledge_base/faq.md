# Frequently Asked Questions

## How long does implementation take?

A working prototype is usually ready in 1–2 weeks. Production deployment typically takes 4–8 weeks depending on integrations and conversation complexity.

## Do you work with non-technical clients?

Yes. We handle architecture, integrations, deployment, and handover. You only need to provide business requirements, access to tools, and feedback during testing.

## Which messengers and channels do you support?

Telegram, WhatsApp (via Business API partners), website chat widgets, email, and custom API endpoints. Instagram and Slack are available on request.

## Which CRMs do you integrate with?

HubSpot, Bitrix24, Zoho, Pipedrive, Salesforce, and custom CRMs via REST API or webhooks.

## Is my data secure?

We use encryption in transit and at rest, environment-based secrets, no hardcoded API keys, and role-based access. On-premise and private-cloud deployments are available for enterprise clients.

## Can the agent speak multiple languages?

Yes. Most projects start with Russian and English; additional languages can be added.

## What if the AI makes a mistake?

Every agent has fallback flows: clarify, hand off to a human, or collect contact details. We also log all interactions for review and continuous improvement.

## Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Maintenance includes monitoring, bug fixes, prompt improvements, monthly analytics reports, and minor scenario updates.

## Can I see a demo?

Yes. Book a 30-minute discovery call via this agent and we will show a live demo tailored to your use case.

## What information do you need for an estimate?

- Channels and approximate monthly volume.
- Current CRM or software.
- Business problem you want to solve.
- Budget range and desired timeline.
- Whether you are the decision maker.
