# Case Studies

## Dental Pro — Dental clinic chain

**Problem**: 1,500+ incoming messages per month across WhatsApp and website. Managers missed leads outside working hours.

**Solution**: AI Sales Agent that qualifies patients, answers pricing questions, checks service availability, and books appointments into the clinic CRM.

**Results**:
- Response time reduced from 4 hours to under 1 minute.
- 35% more leads converted to appointments.
- Managers save ~25 hours per week on initial qualification.

## MetroLogistics — Logistics company

**Problem**: Customers asked the same questions about delivery status, pricing, and pickup points. Support team was overloaded.

**Solution**: RAG-based support assistant integrated with the order tracking API and internal knowledge base.

**Results**:
- 60% of inquiries resolved without human involvement.
- Average support ticket cost dropped by 40%.
- Customer satisfaction score increased by 18%.

## EduStart — Online education platform

**Problem**: Leads from ads were not qualified before reaching sales reps, wasting time on low-intent inquiries.

**Solution**: Lead-processing automation with scoring, enrichment, and CRM routing. AI agent asks key qualification questions before handing off to sales.

**Results**:
- Sales rep productivity increased by 30%.
- Lead qualification accuracy reached 88%.
- Cost per qualified lead decreased by 22%.

## HomeKey — Real estate agency

**Problem**: Agents spent evenings manually answering questions about listings and scheduling viewings.

**Solution**: Telegram bot connected to the listing database and agent calendars. Handles property search, basic filters, and viewing bookings.

**Results**:
- 50+ hours per week saved across the team.
- 20% increase in viewing bookings.
- Agent calendars stay up to date automatically.
