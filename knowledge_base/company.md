# Company Overview

We are an AI automation agency that helps businesses build and deploy production-ready AI agents, assistants, and workflow automations.

## Mission

Make AI automation accessible, reliable, and measurable for SMBs and mid-market companies. We help teams delegate repetitive customer-facing and internal processes to AI agents that actually work.

## What we do

- Design AI agent strategies aligned with business goals.
- Build custom AI Sales Agents, support assistants, and internal process agents.
- Integrate AI with CRMs, messengers, calendars, and internal tools.
- Run RAG-based knowledge systems on company data.
- Provide ongoing monitoring, analytics, and optimization.

## Team

The core team combines backend engineers, ML/LLM engineers, conversation designers, and project managers. Every project is led by a technical lead who owns architecture, quality, and delivery timeline.

## Values

- **Transparency**: no hidden costs; architecture and limitations are discussed upfront.
- **Reliability**: production-grade observability, error handling, and fallback flows.
- **Business outcome first**: we measure success by saved hours, qualified leads, or faster response times, not by model hype.
