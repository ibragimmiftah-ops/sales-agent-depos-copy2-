# Services

## AI Sales Agent

An autonomous agent that handles incoming leads, qualifies them, answers product questions, and books discovery calls. Works in Telegram, WhatsApp, website chat, and email.

## AI Support Assistant

A RAG-powered assistant trained on your knowledge base. Answers common questions, escalates complex cases, and integrates with ticketing systems.

## Lead Processing Automation

End-to-end automation of lead capture, deduplication, enrichment, scoring, and routing to sales reps. Reduces response time from hours to seconds.

## Telegram Bots

Custom Telegram bots for lead capture, order status, notifications, surveys, and internal workflows. Includes admin panels and analytics.

## AI Agents for Business Operations

Internal agents for HR screening, document processing, invoice reconciliation, inventory checks, and data entry.

## RAG Systems

Build Retrieval-Augmented Generation systems on top of your documents, wikis, PDFs, and databases. Includes chunking, embeddings, metadata filtering, and citation.

## CRM Integrations

Two-way integrations with HubSpot, Bitrix24, Zoho, Salesforce, Pipedrive, and custom CRMs via API. Keeps lead data in sync between AI agent and CRM.

## Process Automation Consulting

Audit of current workflows, ROI estimation, automation roadmap, and implementation plan.

## Typical Deliverables

- Working agent prototype in 1–2 weeks.
- Production deployment with monitoring in 4–8 weeks.
- Documentation and handover for internal teams.
